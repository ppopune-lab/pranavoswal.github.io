Who I am

Built in finance. Curious about everything around it.

I like difficult work, clean thinking, and ideas that can actually move people or organizations.

I cleared CA Foundation with AIR-38, then cleared Intermediate and Final in the first attempt every time. That kind of rigor is still the backbone of how I work.

I’m currently a CFA Level III Candidate. But the bigger story is that I’ve never wanted to stay boxed into one narrow lane of finance.

I started at PwC in statutory audit across clients with turnover between ₹400 Cr and ₹6,000 Cr. Then I moved to Mondelez, where I worked on capex and manufacturing operations. After that, I joined Motilal Oswal as Investor Relations Manager, spending my time on investor communication, market-facing narratives, benchmarking, and public-market context.

Now I’m back at Prakash M Oswal & Co. as a consultant, helping professionalize my father’s firm and push it toward stronger advisory work, sharper systems, and a much more modern operating standard.

A big part of how I think was shaped outside India too. I spent a year in Brazil as a Rotary Youth Exchange Ambassador, learned Portuguese well enough to present in it, and addressed 1,500+ people at a Rotary District Conference. That experience made me calmer in unfamiliar rooms and much better at connecting across cultures.

I speak English, Hindi, Marwadi, Marathi, Portuguese, and basic Spanish. I’m obsessed with finance, strategy, content, and startups because all four reward clarity — and I like being the person who can bring clarity into the room.
